# Massachusetts Economy 2026

Sources for a factual brief on the Massachusetts economy, current as of June 24, 2026. By Tim Miano.

Read the brief on Estate of Bits: https://estateofbits.substack.com/p/massachusetts-economy-2026

## What is here

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `massachusetts-economy-2026.md` | The brief as published, with its 73-entry bibliography |
| `sources.csv` | The bibliography as data: number, citation, URL, link-check status and date, notes, and a replacement URL where the original is dead |
| `statements-of-fact.md` | The innovation-economy statements (life sciences, quantum, federal research and defense, fusion, robotics, and others), each with its source linked inline |

## Link check

All 73 URLs were requested on the date in `sources.csv`:

- 60 returned 200.
- 10 returned 403 because the site blocks automated requests (mass.gov, Justia, the Institute for Justice, the U.S. EDA, Harvard Quantum Initiative). They open in a browser.
- 2 returned 404 and have replacements in `replacement_url`: the DARPA/QuEra Stage B press release (no archived copy found; replaced with a report of the same selection) and the UMass Boston quantum-collaborative announcement (moved by UMass Boston).
- FRED timed out in the automated check; an archived copy is noted.

## Limits

- The brief cites its sources in one bibliography rather than claim by claim. `statements-of-fact.md` gives claim-level sourcing for the innovation-economy sections; the other sections (output, migration, housing and energy costs, business formation, the surtax, the founder-exit tax comparison) map to the bibliography entries by subject.
- Figures are as reported by each source at the time. Items described as scheduled or under appeal on June 24, 2026 may have changed since.
