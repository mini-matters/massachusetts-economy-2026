# Massachusetts Innovation Economy — Statements of Fact


## Life sciences

- Massachusetts ranked second among U.S. states in biopharma venture capital in 2025, behind California. ([MassBio 2025 Funding & Pipeline Report](https://www.massbio.org/industry-reports/funding-report/))
- Massachusetts biopharma companies raised \$6.85 billion in venture capital in 2025, equal to 26.2% of U.S. biopharma venture dollars. ([MassBio](https://www.massbio.org/industry-reports/funding-report/))
- The 2025 total was the lowest since 2019 and the fourth consecutive annual decline. ([MassBio](https://www.massbio.org/industry-reports/funding-report/))
- California led with 43.3% of U.S. biopharma venture dollars in 2025. ([MassBio](https://www.massbio.org/industry-reports/funding-report/))
- In 2024, Massachusetts biopharma firms raised \$7.89 billion, 28.3% of U.S. venture dollars. ([MassBio](https://www.massbio.org/industry-reports/funding-report/))
- The number of drugs in development by Massachusetts firms grew approximately 14% year over year in 2025. ([MassBio](https://www.massbio.org/news/recent-news/massachusetts-biopharma-pipeline-surges-14-percent/))
- Kendall Square in Cambridge is described as the densest square mile of technology and biotechnology research; companies based there include Moderna, Biogen, Pfizer, and Takeda. Moderna produced an mRNA COVID-19 vaccine. ([Wikipedia, “Kendall Square”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Square))
- Greater Boston laboratory space availability reached a record of approximately 29.7% in the second quarter of 2025, and newly constructed lab space has been delivered without tenants. ([Boston Real Estate Times](https://bostonrealestatetimes.com/bostons-life-sciences-market-faces-unprecedented-vacancy-as-supply-outpaces-demand/); [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/05/15/business/lab-space-boston-vacant-real-estate-biotech/))

## Quantum computing

- NVIDIA announced in March 2025 that it would site its Accelerated Quantum Computing Research Center in Boston. ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-to-build-accelerated-quantum-computing-research-center))
- QuEra, a Boston quantum-computing company spun out of Harvard and MIT, raised a \$230 million round in 2025. ([QuEra](https://www.quera.com/press-releases/quera-expands-230-million-financing-round-advancing-quantum-accelerated-supercomputing))
- QuEra was selected for Stage B of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative in November 2025. ([DARPA / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/darpa-selects-quera-for-stage-b-of-quantum-benchmarking-initiative-302606186.html))
- In May 2026, Massachusetts committed up to \$25 million toward a new MIT Quantum Systems Laboratory. ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/news/governor-healey-announces-25-million-for-new-mit-quantum-systems-lab-strengthening-mas-global-leadership-in-quantum-computing-research))
- MIT operates a Center for Quantum Engineering that engages more than 80 principal investigators across the MIT campus and Lincoln Laboratory. ([MIT Center for Quantum Engineering](https://cqe.mit.edu/))

## Federal research and defense institutions

- MIT Lincoln Laboratory, in Lexington, is a Department of Defense federally funded research and development center and is described as the largest U.S. defense research-and-prototyping FFRDC; it has approximately 4,500 staff. ([Wikipedia, “MIT Lincoln Laboratory”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Lincoln_Laboratory))
- MIT's Air Force contract to operate Lincoln Laboratory was valued at up to \$12.2 billion (announced April 2025; running through 2030). ([ExecutiveGov](https://executivegov.com/2025/04/mit-12b-air-force-contract-lincoln-lab-rnd-center-operation/))
- Draper, a Cambridge engineering organization, holds a potential five-year, \$2.2 billion U.S. Navy contract for the Trident II Mark 6 guidance subsystem. ([Draper](https://www.draper.com/media-center/news-releases/detail/23450/draper-awarded-contract-to-support-development-of-next-generation-trident-ii-weapon-system-under-2-2b-navy-contract))
- Draper's annual revenue is approximately \$0.8–0.9 billion. ([ProPublica, Form 990](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/42505372))

## Fusion energy

- Commonwealth Fusion Systems, headquartered in Devens, Massachusetts, is a spin-out of MIT. ([Commonwealth Fusion Systems](https://cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-raises-863-million-series-b2-round-to-accelerate-the-commercialization-of-fusion-energy/))
- Commonwealth Fusion raised an \$863 million Series B2 round in August 2025, bringing total funding to close to \$3 billion, described as about one-third of all private fusion capital raised worldwide. ([Commonwealth Fusion Systems](https://cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-raises-863-million-series-b2-round-to-accelerate-the-commercialization-of-fusion-energy/))
- Google signed a power-purchase agreement with Commonwealth Fusion Systems. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/30/google-partnership-commonwealth-fusion-systems.html))
- Commonwealth Fusion Systems' first commercial power plant, ARC, is sited in Chesterfield County, Virginia. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/30/google-partnership-commonwealth-fusion-systems.html); [Canary Media](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/nuclear/commonwealth-fusion-systems-series-b2-funding))

## Artificial intelligence and venture capital

- Global AI venture capital in 2025 was approximately \$211 billion. ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/big-funding-trends-charts-eoy-2025/))
- The San Francisco Bay Area received approximately 60% (about \$126 billion) of 2025 AI venture capital. ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/big-funding-trends-charts-eoy-2025/); [The Letter Two](https://thelettertwo.com/2026/02/07/ai-venture-capital-2025-san-francisco))
- Greater Boston received an estimated 3–4% of AI venture capital in 2025. ([The Letter Two](https://thelettertwo.com/2026/02/07/ai-venture-capital-2025-san-francisco))
- OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI are headquartered in the San Francisco Bay Area. ([San Francisco Examiner](https://www.sfexaminer.com/news/technology/why-venture-capital-just-couldnt-quit-san-francisco-in-2025/article_1fd1195f-3776-473d-b0f3-389f5a740c02.html))
- Liquid AI is a Boston foundation-model company spun out of MIT's CSAIL; it raised a \$250 million Series A at a \$2.35 billion valuation in December 2025. ([Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/we-raised-250m-to-scale-capable-and-efficient-general-purpose-ai); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/liquid-ai))
- In the first quarter of 2026, startups in Texas raised more venture capital (\$5.8 billion) than startups in Massachusetts (\$5.3 billion); reporting described this as the first time on record. ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/04/15/business/vc-startups-massachusetts-texas-funding/))
- General Catalyst, a venture firm with Massachusetts roots, acquired a hospital system and has stated it is considering an initial public offering; it has described itself as an “investment and transformation company.” ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/03/general-catalyst-loses-three-top-investors-as-the-firm-expands-beyond-venture-contemplates-ipo/))

([WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/news/2025/12/15/irobot-bankruptcy-picea-restructuring-massachusetts); [Manufacturing Dive](https://www.manufacturingdive.com/news/roomba-braava-maker-irobot-chapter-11-bankruptcy-acquisition-picea-china/807997/))

## Cost of doing business and housing

- CNBC's 2025 “America's Top States for Business” ranked Massachusetts 20th overall and as the most-improved state; it also ranked Massachusetts the second-most-expensive state in which to do business, with the highest wage costs in the country and the third-highest utility costs. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/10/top-states-for-business-massachusetts.html))
- The Tax Foundation's 2026 State Tax Competitiveness Index ranked Massachusetts 43rd. ([Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/state/2026-state-tax-competitiveness-index/))
- The median single-family home price in greater Boston passed \$1 million in 2025. ([Axios Boston](https://www.axios.com/local/boston/2025/07/21/median-home-price-1-million))
- The income required to buy a lower-priced home in greater Boston rose from approximately \$98,000 in 2021 to approximately \$162,000 in 2025. ([Axios Boston / Boston Foundation](https://www.axios.com/local/boston/2025/11/12/greater-boston-housing-report-card))

## Taxation

- Massachusetts voters approved a 4% surtax on annual income above \$1 million (the “Fair Share” amendment) in 2022. ([Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/economy/democratic-states-wealthy-tax-bills-c924b3b5))
- The surtax has raised approximately \$3 billion per year, above initial forecasts. ([Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/economy/democratic-states-wealthy-tax-bills-c924b3b5))
- The number of net-worth millionaires resident in Massachusetts grew approximately 38.6% from 2022 to 2024. ([Axios Boston](https://www.axios.com/local/boston/2025/04/29/millionaires-massachusetts-income-surtax-increase))
- Cristobal Young's study *The Myth of Millionaire Tax Flight* (Stanford University Press, 2017) found that millionaires migrate at lower rates than the general population. ([Review of the evidence](https://www.peoplespolicyproject.org/2025/11/17/do-millionaire-surtaxes-lead-to-millionaire-exodus/))

## State and federal policy actions

- The Mass Leads Act, signed in 2024, authorized approximately \$3.5–4 billion for life sciences, artificial intelligence, climatetech, and advanced manufacturing. ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/news/governor-healey-signs-economic-development-bill-to-strengthen-massachusetts-global-leadership-in-climatetech-life-sciences-and-ai))
- A federal policy capping NIH indirect research-cost reimbursement at 15% was struck down; the First Circuit Court of Appeals affirmed the block on January 5, 2026. ([STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/01/05/federal-appeals-court-upholds-blocking-nih-indirect-cost-cuts/); [Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/01/first-circuit-affirms-a-lower-court-decision-prohibiting-nih-from-capping-reimbursements))
- A federal court in Massachusetts struck down a \$100,000 fee on new H-1B visas on June 8, 2026. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/09/nx-s1-5851474/federal-judge-fee-h1b-visa); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/08/politics/federal-judge-voids-trumps-usd100-000-fee-requirement-for-h-1b-visas))
- Medicaid changes in the 2025 federal reconciliation law are projected to reduce Massachusetts hospital funding by billions of dollars per year. ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/07/01/business/hospital-health-care-medical-massachusetts-congress-medicaid-big-beautiful-bill/))
- A 100% tariff on branded, patented pharmaceuticals is scheduled to take effect July 31, 2026. ([Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/04/100-on-brand-us-imposes-new-tariffs-and-key-exemptions-on-patented-pharmaceuticals))
- On December 22, 2025, the U.S. Department of the Interior paused five in-progress offshore-wind projects, including Vineyard Wind, citing national-security grounds. ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/climate-environment/2025/12/22/offshore-wind-lease-pause-vineyard/))

## Historical record

- In the 1980s, technology firms along Route 128 near Boston — including Digital Equipment Corporation, Wang Laboratories, and Data General — were major makers of minicomputers. ([Saxenian, *Regional Advantage*, Harvard University Press, 1994](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674753402))
- AnnaLee Saxenian, in *Regional Advantage* (Harvard University Press, 1994), documented the divergence between Route 128 and Silicon Valley and attributed Silicon Valley's relative advantage to greater mobility of people and information among firms. ([Harvard University Press](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674753402))
